Good to know before you call

Phone calls locally answered 24/7

Our phones are forwarded to a team member each day so we can best help with your questions. At busy times you may get our voicemail. Just leave a quick message and we'll return your call promptly.

Regular hours & emergency after-hours service

Monday to Friday (holidays excluded), 8am to 5pm. Saturday and Sunday by appointment. "Emergency" after-hours situations can need immediate attention; if you're unsure, call to discuss it with our team. Please remember your after-hours call is answered by a team member on their personal time.

Service rates

Diagnostic/dispatch fees are fairly standard across the industry and typically range from $120 to $145 + taxes depending on the area. Be cautious of lower upfront rates, which can be a lure into larger costs down the road.

Safety: gas smell or carbon monoxide alarm

If you smell natural gas or propane, contact your fuel supplier immediately (Enbridge: 1-877-969-0999). For a carbon monoxide alarm, get everyone out to fresh air and call 911.

Choosing the right contractor

Check the HRAI Contractor Locator, ask friends and family, and ask for licences, WSIB and liability insurance. Make sure installers are employees of the company you buy from and that it has a physical location you can visit.
